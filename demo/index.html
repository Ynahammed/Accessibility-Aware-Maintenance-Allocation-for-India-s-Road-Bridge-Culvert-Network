2 · Why network-awareness: the non-additivity explorer

On the redundant testbed each corridor joins a habitation to the clinic by N parallel routes, one structure per route. Fail any set of structures below and watch the true set-based loss L(F) — solo losses stay at zero until a corridor loses all its routes. An additive model prices each structure at its solo loss, sees zero benefit, and funds nothing; the network-aware objective sees the joint risk.
