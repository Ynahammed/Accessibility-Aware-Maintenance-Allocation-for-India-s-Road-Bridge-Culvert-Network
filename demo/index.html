3 · Why network-awareness: the non-additivity explorer

On the redundant testbed each corridor joins a habitation to the clinic by N parallel routes, one structure per route. Fail any set of structures below and watch the true set-based loss L(F) — solo losses stay at zero until a corridor loses all its routes. An additive model prices each structure at its solo loss, sees zero benefit, and funds nothing; the network-aware objective sees the joint risk.

Lower loss is better. Negative condition-first − random values favour condition-first; the final column is the additive policy’s estimated extra reduction versus condition-first.

Scroll horizontally to compare both districts
In these frozen runs condition-first beats random on average in both districts; that is the opposite of “practice is near chance.” The estimated additive increment over condition-first is smaller, but this is not evidence of equivalence: draws share one assumed condition/cost setup, crossings are unverified, and the 40-draw comparison does not validate real-world maintenance outcomes. Paired intervals below are reconstructed from the stored paired standard deviation (Student t, 39 degrees of freedom).
