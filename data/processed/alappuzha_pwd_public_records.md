## Download attempt and gap

The Government of Kerala document portal returned HTTP 503 for both direct PWD order PDFs from this machine, including after retries; no PDF copy is present in this workspace. Three official eTender detail pages were downloaded as HTML into `data/raw/pwd/alappuzha_bridge_culvert_orders/` and checked for their tender IDs. Their listed work/tender values are procurement records, not an asset-level bridge/culvert condition-and-cost export.

Kerala PWD's [PRICE asset-management description](https://price.kerala.gov.in/) says asset registers can include bridges and culverts, geocoding, and work histories tagged to each asset; it also states that asset changes depend on user privileges and approval workflows. The [PWD asset page](https://www.pwd.kerala.gov.in/IMF_website/About_us/assets.php?id=ODk=&id_ml=RU4=) links to summary asset information, while the [iROADS citizen portal](https://pwdrmms.kerala.gov.in/citizen/portal) provides public road/DLP information rather than a downloadable bridge condition inventory.

The requested PWD CSV/spreadsheet/GIS export—asset ID, coordinates or chainage, type, inspection date, condition/rating, restrictions, ownership and estimated rehabilitation cost—remains unavailable from these public pages. The remaining path is a request to the PWD Bridges division, followed if needed by an RTI request for non-personal records.
